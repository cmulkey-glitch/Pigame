## Online description

Spiral Shift is a fast, simple-to-play runner that takes you around every edge of your TV
screen.

Every level is four sides of the screen, and every side changes how you play:
- Ball (along the bottom): press to jump, hold to keep bouncing.
- Triangle (up the right side): steer with the arrows; it stays put when you let go.
- Square (along the top): flip gravity between the edge and the ceiling.
- Diamond (down the left side): hold to cut diagonally inward, let go to cut back.

At every corner your shape becomes a rocket, blasts around the bend and lands as the next shape.
Each corner is also a checkpoint, so a crash only sends you back to the start of the side you're
on.

The levels wind inward like a real spiral: beyond the side you're running, you can see the next
level waiting, each one shorter and faster than the last. Play any level on its own, or take on
the Spiral Run and travel through all ten layers without stopping, right into the core at the
centre. Pause any time; your spiral run is saved when you quit.

Random worlds build a fresh ten-layer spiral from a five-letter code, with the shapes on new
sides. Today's world is free and the same for everyone, so you can race your friends for the
best time.

Spiral Shift is free with occasional ads. One purchase unlocks the full version: millions of
random worlds, replay any world, and no ads.

- 10 levels, from Easy to Insane, plus a free world of the day
- 4 shapes, each with its own controls
- Easy mode slows the whole game down without changing the levels
- Best time and fewest attempts saved for every level, spiral run and world
- Original music and art
- Plays with your Roku remote: OK or any arrow for the ball and diamond, the arrows for the
  triangle and the square
