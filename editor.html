CHAMBER COLOURS

Up to 4 treasures and 4 keys per chamber. A key opens its door the moment it's picked up, in any chamber. "Start here" sets where play-tests begin in this chamber (not saved).

Click near the left or right wall to add a door at that row, or use the button. Each door is one-way: add one in the other chamber to come back. "Auto" arrival lands you at the door that leads back.

Click to add a drop spawn point (put them at stalactite tips), right-click to remove. Drops fall from random points in the list until they reach a floor.

Saved with the levels; play-test to feel changes live.
